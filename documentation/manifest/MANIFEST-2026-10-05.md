## 6. Decisions made this session

- Removal is permanent for any cause: no reactivation, no suspension. A returning person gets a new invitation and is a new member. Personal information is not kept after removal; the member row and its primary key stay so history is not orphaned; names become Self-deleted User or Deactivated User.
- Removal also detaches the person from their leader and all groups, clears their current Commit/Refuse answers (history kept), prunes them from task assignments, and deletes their unavailability and event view rows.
- Audit log: use member ids, not names or other identifying details; redact existing entries (FP-236, decided, not built).
- Commit/Refuse history is append-only and never deleted; any response (Commit or Refuse) lowers the Tasks badge.
- Needs Attention: owner and Admins only, current refusals from people who still resolve as assignees, live events only. Recently Modified: everyone who has opened the event before on a build that records views, never the editor, never part of a badge; shows what changed as labels (from the audit log; a version bump without an audit entry shows as Tasks).
- Mobile cards use full-width red and amber banner strips with icon and bold text, never color alone.
- A task that loses its only assignee has no signal of its own beyond the Tasks label.
- RSVP roster (FP-240, after a community poll): every person who can open an event sees the full roster with names, responses and guest counts, on the mobile Event Detail only. Decline reasons are visible only to Admin tier, the decliner's own leader and the decliner. Removed members are not shown in that roster. Reports, web admin pages and announcement acknowledgement rosters are unchanged. The web admin event page keeps its old roster behavior through ?view=admin (tech debt).
- Push notifications are out of scope (FP-227 deferred).
- Billing (FP-172) is a draft design only; see section 7b.

Provider (Joseph asked): Stripe recommended (Checkout, Billing, Customer Portal; Stripe Tax later, only when tax registration is needed). Keep Stripe behind a thin adapter so it stays swappable. Joseph should confirm tax registration questions with an accountant (small supplier threshold of about C$30,000 in Canada; US states differ).
