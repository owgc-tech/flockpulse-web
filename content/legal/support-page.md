# FlockPulse Support

**FlockPulse** is operated by Joseph Punzalan, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Contact us

Email [support@flockpulse.ca](mailto:support@flockpulse.ca). We reply within 3 business days. This is a small team, so please be patient.

FlockPulse is not for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

## What to include

- What happened, and what you expected to happen.
- The name of your Community.
- Whether you use the web app or the mobile app (iOS or Android).
- A screenshot, if it helps. Please do not send your password.

## Common questions

**I forgot my password.** You can reset it yourself. This works for everyone: Members, Leaders and Community Administrators.

*In the mobile app*

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. A page opens in your phone's browser. Type your email.
3. We send you an email with a reset link. It comes from admin@flockpulse.ca ("FlockPulse Admin").
4. Open the link and choose a new password.
5. Tap **Back to the app**. Then sign in with your new password.

*On the web*

Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?** The steps are the same.

Good to know:

- Each link works one time only. It also stops working after a short time.
- If you ask for a new link, the old link stops working. Always use the newest email.
- After you save a new password, you are signed out on all your devices. Sign in again.
- The page gives the same answer if your email is in FlockPulse or not. This protects everyone's privacy. If no email comes, look in your spam folder. Check that you typed the email you use for FlockPulse. If you still have no email, write to us.
- If you are signed in to the mobile app, you can also change your password on the **Edit Profile** screen.

**I lost my authenticator app.** The mobile app needs a two-step login for every account, and the web admin area needs it for Leaders and Community Administrators. Email us from the address on your account. We will check that it is you first.

**I never got my invitation.** Check your spam folder. Then ask your Community Administrator to send it again.

**Questions about your Community's events, programs or rules.** Ask your Community Administrator. We run the software, but your Community runs its own programs.

**I want to delete my account.** See [Delete your FlockPulse account](account-deletion-page.md).

**I want to see, correct or ask about my personal information.** Email us. We will confirm we received your request within 5 business days and answer within 30 days. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**I want to report a problem with content.** Email us. Tell us what it is and where to find it. See section 7 of our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

**I found a security problem.** Email us. Please do not exploit it or test it against other people's data.
