# Delete your FlockPulse account

**FlockPulse** is operated by Joseph Punzalan, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

You can delete your FlockPulse account and the information tied to it. You do not need the app installed to ask. Deleting is permanent. There is no undo, and you cannot reactivate the account. If you come back later, you join as a new Member.

## Option 1: Delete in the app

1. Open the FlockPulse mobile app.
2. Tap your initials (top right).
3. Tap **Edit Profile**.
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete My Account** (the red button).
5. Confirm.

The deletion happens right away.

## Option 2: Ask us by email

Use this option if you cannot use the app or no longer have it.

1. Email [support@flockpulse.ca](mailto:support@flockpulse.ca?subject=Delete%20my%20FlockPulse%20account) with the subject "Delete my FlockPulse account".
2. Send it from the email address on your account.
3. Tell us your name and your Community's name.

We will check that the request is really from you. We may ask you one or two questions to be sure. We will confirm that we received your request within 5 business days. We will finish within 30 days, and we will tell you when it is done.

## If you are a Community Administrator

- A Community Administrator can remove a Member from the Members page on the web. The removal works the same way as the steps above. Only another Administrator can remove an Administrator.
- The last Administrator of a Community can never be removed, and cannot delete their own account, until another Member is made an Administrator. If you are the only Administrator, make another Member an Administrator first, or email us to close the Community.
- Deleting your own account does not close your Community. To close a Community, email us. We will send you a copy of its data first, if you ask. We will then delete all of the Community's data within 60 days after the Community ends.

## If you lead Members, own a group or own an event

Your account cannot be deleted while you lead active Members, own active groups, or own upcoming events (events that are not cancelled or finished). Past events do not block it. The app shows a message that says what to hand over first. Ask your Community Administrator to reassign them, or email us and we will ask the Administrator for you. The same checks apply to email requests and to removal by a Community Administrator.

## What is deleted

- Your login, password and two-step login setup.
- Your name. It becomes "Self-deleted User" (or "Deactivated User" if an Administrator removed you).
- Your email address. It is replaced by a placeholder, including on invitations.
- Your gender and marital status.
- Your availability dates and "what changed" tracking.
- Your links to your Leader and your groups.
- Your task assignments and your current Commit or Refuse answers.
- Free text you wrote in RSVP reasons, attendance reasons and Leader notes. It becomes `[removed]`.

## What stays, and why

A de-identified record stays under an internal member ID. It holds your role, your account creation date, your RSVP answers and guest counts, your attendance records and dates, your program completions, your announcement acknowledgements, your event invitations, your past Commit or Refuse answers, your record of accepting the Terms and Privacy Policy (your Community, the effective date of each document you accepted, your 18+ confirmation, and the time), and audit log entries that show only the member ID.

We keep the attendance records so that your Community's attendance history and statistics stay correct. We keep the acceptance record, with no name, so we can show that you accepted the Terms and the Privacy Policy and confirmed that you are 18 or older if there is a dispute. It has no name or email.

**De-identified does not mean anonymous.** We removed your name, contact details and free text. In a small Community, someone who knows the Community may still work out who a record belongs to. We cannot promise that the remaining record cannot be linked to you.

Event feedback you gave was never stored with your name, so it is not part of your account and we cannot find it to delete it.

## What is not cleared right away

Copies in backups, in logs kept by our hosting providers and by our email provider, and invitation emails already in someone's inbox. These clear on their own schedules. Section 10 of our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) has the details.
