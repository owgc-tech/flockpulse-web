# FlockPulse Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-21
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

## Summary

FlockPulse is a service that helps a Community (such as a club, a non-profit, a faith community or an alumni group) run its events, attendance, programs and tasks. This Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, where it is kept, how long we keep it, and how you can ask questions or delete your account.

The main points:

- Your Community decides what it needs to know about its Members. FlockPulse handles that information for the Community. We also keep some information for our own purposes, such as your login and our security.
- We do not sell or rent personal information. We show no ads. We use no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
- Feedback you give after an event is stored without any link to you.
- You must be 18 or older to use FlockPulse. You confirm this with a tick box when you register. We do not ask for your birthdate.
- If you delete your account, it is permanent. We erase your name, email, login and free-text answers. A de-identified record stays so that your Community's history stays correct. De-identified does not mean anonymous. See section 10.
- You can contact us at support@flockpulse.ca.

## 1. Who we are and key words

FlockPulse is operated by Joseph Punzalan, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada. (A sole proprietor is a person who runs a business under their own name, without a company.) In this Policy:

- **"FlockPulse," "we" and "us"** mean the FlockPulse service and the person who operates it.
- **"Service"** means the FlockPulse web app, the FlockPulse mobile apps for iOS and Android, and the website at flockpulse.ca.
- **"Community"** means an organization that has its own private space in FlockPulse. Communities cannot see each other's data.
- **"Community Administrator"** means a person who runs a Community's space.
- **"Leader"** means a Member who leads other Members and confirms their attendance.
- **"Member"** means a person invited into a Community.
- **"You"** means anyone who uses the Service.
- **"Personal information"** means information about an identifiable person.

We may later move FlockPulse to a company. If we do, this Policy will name the new legal entity (see section 16).

## 2. Two roles: your Community and FlockPulse

In privacy law, the organization that decides why and how personal information is used is responsible for it. An organization that only handles information for another organization is often called a "service provider" (or "processor").

- **Member information.** Your Community decides why it collects information about its Members and how it uses it. This covers your profile, events, RSVPs, attendance, program progress, tasks, availability and announcements. FlockPulse handles this information for the Community. We follow the Community's instructions and our agreement with it.
- **FlockPulse's own information.** We decide how to use the information for your login account, the security of the Service, and our support inbox. We are responsible for that information. Billing information will be in this group if we start billing Communities.

What this means for you:

- If you want to know why your Community collects something, ask your Community Administrator first. We can also help you reach the right person.
- Your Community is responsible for telling its Members how it uses their information and for getting any consent the law requires.

## 3. What information we collect

### When you register

- Your first and last name and your email address.
- Your password. Our login provider stores it in scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot read it.
- Your gender and marital status, which you enter on your profile.
- Your role (Community Administrator, Leader or Member), your group, and who your Leader is.
- A record that you accepted our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) and this Policy. It holds your member ID, your Community, the effective date of each document you accepted, your 18+ confirmation, and the time. It has no name or email. When you register, you accept both documents with a tick box. You also tick a box to confirm that you are 18 or older. That is your own confirmation. We do not check it and we do not ask for proof of age.

We do not collect your birthdate or your phone number. Before November 21, 2026, FlockPulse asked for a birthdate. On November 21, 2026, we permanently deleted every stored birthdate, so we hold no birthdate for anyone. Backups keep older copies until they expire (section 10). A Community that keeps a birthday list keeps it outside FlockPulse, and is responsible for it.

### Two-step login

The FlockPulse mobile app requires a two-step login, with an authenticator app, for all accounts. The web admin area requires it for Leaders and Community Administrators. We store the setup for that app with your login. In the web admin area we also set a cookie that remembers, for a set number of days, that you passed the second step in this browser. The default is 28 days.

### Events and participation

- **Events:** name, description, date and time, place name and street address, online meeting link, type of event, and who is invited.
- **RSVP before an event:** Yes, No or Tentative, a reason if you answer No, and the number of guests.
- **Attendance report after an event:** whether you came, and a reason if you did not.
- **Official attendance:** whether a Leader or Community Administrator recorded that you attended, who confirmed it, when, and any note the Leader added.
- **Program progress:** which course sessions you completed. This comes only from official attendance.
- **Tasks:** tasks assigned to you, your Commit or Refuse answers, and the history of those answers.
- **Announcements:** whether you acknowledged an announcement, and when.
- **Availability:** dates you say you are not available. These stop tasks being assigned to you.
- **"What changed" tracking:** when you last viewed events, so the app can show what is new.

### Feedback after an event

You can add a comment of up to 1,000 characters and a star rating from 1 to 5. See section 7 for how this is stored.

### Information about Communities and invitations

- **Content written by Community Administrators:** the Community's name, tagline, description, page text, announcements, event descriptions and the text of invitation emails. A Community's logo is stored in a public location, so anyone with the link can see it.
- **Invitations:** before you join, we hold your email address, the role and group you were invited to, who invited you, and the date and status of the invitation.

### Support

When you email support@flockpulse.ca, we keep your message and your email address in our support inbox.

### Audit log

We keep a log of actions by Community Administrators and the system, so that changes can be traced. Entries about people record the member ID only, not names or emails. Entries about other things, such as events or groups, can include text that a Community Administrator typed. Only Community Administrators can read the audit log.

### Technical information

- **Server logs.** Our hosting providers keep logs of requests to the Service. These may include your IP address, your browser or device type, and the time of the request.
- **On your phone.** The app stores your login session, your reminder settings, the time of your last check for changes, and your theme. If you turn on Face ID or fingerprint unlock, the app stores only an on/off flag. Your phone does the face or fingerprint check. We never receive your face or fingerprint data.
- **App updates.** When the app checks for an update, our update provider (Expo) receives the app version and your device type.
- **Browser storage.** See section 11.

### What we do not collect

- No phone numbers or birthdates.
- No location tracking. Event addresses are typed in by Community Administrators and Leaders. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.
- No advertising data, and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- run the Service for your Community: sign you in, send invitations and password-reset emails, show events, record RSVPs and attendance, track program progress, assign tasks, and let Community Administrators and Leaders see the reports they are allowed to see;
- keep the Service secure and find and fix problems, including through the audit log;
- answer your support emails and your privacy requests;
- meet our legal duties.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

## 5. Who can see your information inside a Community

Access is controlled by role. The server checks your role before it shows any data.

- **Community Administrators** can see Member information in their Community: names, emails, gender, marital status, roles, groups, RSVPs, attendance, program progress, availability, and the audit log.
- **Leaders** can see the Members they lead, and the events they own, are invited to or are assigned to.
- **Members** can see their own information.
- **Event rosters.** People who can open an event can see who RSVP'd. Removed Members are hidden.
- **RSVP reasons.** Visible only to you, your own Leader and Community Administrators.
- **Attendance reports.** Your reason for not attending is visible only to you, your own Leader and Community Administrators.
- **Names of people who refused a task.** Visible only to the event owner and Community Administrators.
- **Announcement acknowledgements.** Visible to Community Administrators and the person who owns the announcement.
- **Feedback.** Shown on the Board tab of the mobile app, without any name. No one can see who wrote it (section 7).
- **Reminders on your phone.** A reminder's text can include the event name. It can appear on your lock screen, where other people may see it.

Other Communities cannot see anything about your Community.

## 6. Sensitive information

FlockPulse works for any kind of Community, including faith communities. Belonging to a Community, attending its events and completing its programs can show a person's beliefs or personal life. Your gender and marital status may also decide which programs a Community offers you. We treat this kind of information as sensitive.

- We do not ask you for your religion or beliefs.
- We limit who can see this information by role (section 5).
- We use it only to run the Service.
- Your Community is responsible for telling you why it collects this information and for getting your consent, including express (clear and direct) consent where the law requires it.

## 7. Feedback

Feedback is a comment and a star rating that you can give after an event.

- Feedback is stored with the event it is about. It is not stored with your name, your account or the time you wrote it.
- No one can see who wrote it. That includes your Community Administrators, your Leaders and us.
- Our hosting providers keep their own technical logs (section 3). We do not use those logs to find out who wrote feedback.
- Because we do not know which feedback is yours, we cannot find, change or delete it later.
- Please do not put your name or other identifying details in a comment. In a small group, details can give you away.

## 10. How long we keep information, and deleting your account

### How long we keep information

- **Your Member record:** while you are a Member of an active Community, until you or a Community Administrator remove you.
- **Audit log:** while the Community uses FlockPulse.
- **Support emails:** about 2 years after the last message, then we delete them.
- **Invitations that were never accepted:** until a Community Administrator cancels them or the Community ends.
- **Hosting logs:** Supabase keeps API and database logs for 7 days. Vercel shows its runtime logs for 1 day and keeps some logs longer for its own purposes. It does not publish how long. Mailtrap keeps its own record of the emails it sends.
- **Backups:** deleted information can remain in backups until those backups are replaced on their normal schedule.

### How to delete your account

- **In the mobile app:** tap your initials (top right), then **Edit Profile**, then **Delete My Account** (at the bottom).
- **By a Community Administrator:** Administrators can remove a Member from the Members page on the web. Only another Administrator can remove an Administrator.
- **By email:** if you cannot use the app, use the Account Deletion page on flockpulse.ca or email support@flockpulse.ca. We will confirm your identity, then do the same removal.

Removal is blocked while you lead active Members, own active groups, or own upcoming events (events that are not cancelled or finished). Past events do not block it. The app shows a plain message that says what to hand over first. Your Community Administrator can reassign these for you. The same checks apply when you ask by email and when a Community Administrator removes you.

The last Community Administrator of a Community can never be removed, and cannot delete their own account, until another Member is made an Administrator. If you are the only Administrator, make another Member an Administrator first, or email us to close the Community.

Deleting is permanent. You cannot reactivate the account. If you come back, you join as a new Member.

### What happens when you delete your account

- **Erased or deleted:**
  - Your login, password and two-step login setup.
  - Your name, which becomes "Self-deleted User" (or "Deactivated User" if an Administrator removed you).
  - Your email address, which is replaced by a placeholder, including on invitations.
  - Your gender and marital status.
  - Your availability dates and "what changed" tracking.
  - Your links to your Leader and your groups.
  - Your task assignments, and your current Commit or Refuse answers.
  - Free text you wrote in RSVP reasons, attendance reasons and Leader notes. This text becomes `[removed]`, including inside the audit log.
- **Stays, de-identified:** a record under an internal member ID. It holds your role, your account creation date, your RSVP answers and guest counts, your attendance records and dates, your program completions, your announcement acknowledgements, your event invitations, your past Commit or Refuse answers, your record of accepting the [Terms](terms-of-service.md) and this Policy (your Community, the effective date of each document you accepted, your 18+ confirmation, and the time), and audit log entries that show only the member ID.
- **Why it stays:** so your Community's attendance history and statistics stay correct. The record of accepting the Terms and Policy stays so that we can show, if there is a dispute, that you accepted the Terms of Service and this Policy and confirmed that you are 18 or older. It has no name or email attached.
- **De-identified does not mean anonymous.** We removed your name, contact details and free text. But in a small Community, someone who knows the Community may still work out who a record belongs to. We cannot promise that the remaining record cannot be linked to you.

### What deletion does not reach right away

Copies in backups, the logs kept by our hosting providers and by Mailtrap, and invitation and password-reset emails already sitting in someone's inbox. These are cleared on their providers' schedules or by the person who holds them. Anything your Community copied outside FlockPulse is the Community's responsibility.

### When a Community stops using FlockPulse

A Community Administrator can email us to close the Community. On request, we will send a copy of the Community's data first. We will then delete all of the Community's data within 60 days after the Community ends. Backups follow section 10's backup rule.

## 11. Cookies, browser storage and notifications

FlockPulse uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Because the only cookies are the ones needed for login, we do not show a cookie banner.

**On the web app**

| Name | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `sb-…-auth-token.0` and `sb-…-auth-token.1` | Cookies | Keep you signed in. They hold your login session in two parts. |
| `fp_mfa_expires_at` | Cookie | Remembers that a Leader or Community Administrator passed the second login step in this browser (web admin area). It holds only an expiry time. |
| `fp_community_name` | Local storage | Fills in your Community's name on the login screen. It stays until you clear your browser data. |
| `fp_reg_token` and `fp_founder_token` | Session storage | Carry a temporary code between registration steps. They are cleared when you close the tab. |

**On your phone:** reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. FlockPulse does not use push notifications. The app asks your permission to show notifications. You can turn them off in your phone settings at any time.

## 12. Security

We use these safeguards:

- Each Community's data is separated. The server works out your Community from your login, not from what the app sends.
- All data access goes through our server, which checks your role. The database refuses direct access from the apps.
- Two-step login with an authenticator app is required for all accounts in the mobile app, and for Leaders and Community Administrators in the web admin area.
- Traffic to and from the Service is protected with HTTPS.
- Administrator and system actions are written to the audit log.
- We review security updates for the software we use every week.

No service is completely secure. If a breach of security safeguards affects personal information, we will tell the affected Communities. Where the law requires, we will also tell the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the people affected. We keep a record of privacy breaches.

## 13. Age

FlockPulse is for adults. You must be 18 or older. When you register, you must tick a box to confirm that you are 18 or older. Existing Members, Leaders and Community Administrators see a one-time screen with the same box, and with a box to accept the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) and this Policy, the next time they open the app or the web admin area. This is your own confirmation, not proof of age. We do not ask for your birthdate or any proof, and we cannot check your age, so Communities must only invite adults. If we learn that a person under 18 has an account, we will remove it. If you think this has happened, email us.

## 14. Your rights and how to make a request

You can ask to:

- see the personal information we hold about you;
- correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
- delete your account (section 10);
- ask a question, or withdraw a consent you gave.

If you live in Quebec, you can also ask for your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

**Where to send it:**

- For Member information, you can ask your Community Administrator, or ask us. If you ask us, we will either answer or pass your request to your Community.
- For our own information (your login, support emails and security records), ask us.
- Email support@flockpulse.ca. There is no self-service export yet, so requests are handled by email.

**What to expect:**

- We will confirm that we received your request within 5 business days.
- We will answer within 30 days. If we need more time, we will tell you within the 30 days and explain why.
- We may need to confirm who you are first.
- If we cannot give you something, we will tell you why. For example, we cannot find feedback (section 7).

## 15. Complaints

Please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or to your provincial privacy regulator. In Quebec, that is the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

## 16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. For material changes, we will email Community Administrators and show a notice in the app and on flockpulse.ca at least 30 days before the change takes effect. The only exception is an urgent legal or security fix. We will always change the "Last updated" date. If we move FlockPulse to a company, we will name it here.
